The frustrating truth about Spanish irregular verbs: the more common a verb is, the more likely it is to be irregular. The verbs you use the most — *ser*, *ir*, *tener*, *hacer* — are the most unpredictable.

The good news: there are patterns, and the most important verbs are worth memorising because you'll use them constantly.

## Completely Irregular: Ser and Ir

These two are the most irregular verbs in Spanish — and *ser* and *ir* share the same preterite forms (context tells you which is which).

### Ser (to be)

| Tense | yo | tú | él | nosotros | vosotros | ellos |
|-------|----|----|-----|----------|----------|-------|
| Present | soy | eres | es | somos | sois | son |
| Preterite | fui | fuiste | fue | fuimos | fuisteis | fueron |
| Imperfect | era | eras | era | éramos | erais | eran |

### Ir (to go)

| Tense | yo | tú | él | nosotros | vosotros | ellos |
|-------|----|----|-----|----------|----------|-------|
| Present | voy | vas | va | vamos | vais | van |
| Preterite | fui | fuiste | fue | fuimos | fuisteis | fueron |
| Imperfect | iba | ibas | iba | íbamos | ibais | iban |

## Tener — To Have

*Tener* has a *go* verb in the yo present (*tengo*) and is irregular throughout:

| Tense | yo | tú | él | nosotros | vosotros | ellos |
|-------|----|----|-----|----------|----------|-------|
| Present | **tengo** | tienes | tiene | tenemos | tenéis | tienen |
| Preterite | **tuve** | tuviste | tuvo | tuvimos | tuvisteis | tuvieron |
| Future | **tendré** | tendrás | tendrá | tendremos | tendréis | tendrán |

## Poder — To Be Able To

Stem-changing (o→ue in present) plus irregular preterite:

| Tense | yo | tú | él | nosotros | vosotros | ellos |
|-------|----|----|-----|----------|----------|-------|
| Present | **puedo** | **puedes** | **puede** | podemos | podéis | **pueden** |
| Preterite | **pude** | pudiste | pudo | pudimos | pudisteis | pudieron |
| Future | **podré** | podrás | podrá | podremos | podréis | podrán |

## Patterns Worth Knowing

### The "Go" Verbs (Yo Irregular)

Several verbs add *-go* in the *yo* present form but are otherwise regular in the present:

| Verb | Yo form |
|------|---------|
| *tener* | **tengo** |
| *venir* | **vengo** |
| *hacer* | **hago** |
| *decir* | **digo** |
| *poner* | **pongo** |
| *salir* | **salgo** |
| *traer* | **traigo** |
| *caer* | **caigo** |

### Irregular Future/Conditional Stems

Several verbs use a contracted stem in the future and conditional:

| Verb | Future stem |
|------|------------|
| *tener* | tendr- |
| *venir* | vendr- |
| *hacer* | har- |
| *poder* | podr- |
| *querer* | querr- |
| *saber* | sabr- |
| *salir* | saldr- |
| *poner* | pondr- |
